# SoundBeam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

SoundBeam is a free, open-source Android app that plays the sound of other apps on speakers, TVs and phones on your local network. This policy explains what data the app touches and what it does with it. The short version: **SoundBeam has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Nothing leaves your home network unless you send it there.**

## Who we are

SoundBeam is an open-source project published at <https://github.com/ganpatig/SoundBeam>. Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/ganpatig/SoundBeam/issues>.

## What SoundBeam accesses and why

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sound played by other apps** (Android's audio playback capture, which uses the microphone/"record audio" and screen-capture permissions) | To play that sound on the speakers you choose | Only to the speakers, TVs or phones **you select on your local network**, and only while you are streaming. It is not recorded or stored unless you turn on *Record to a file*. SoundBeam never captures your screen image and never uses the microphone itself. |
| **Now-playing information** (song title, artist, album, cover art) via notification access, **only if you allow it** | To show what's playing on speakers that can display it | Only to the speakers/TVs you are streaming to. |
| **Devices on your local network** (speaker names and addresses) | To list speakers you can play to | Stays on your phone. |
| **Your phone's device name** | So a friend can recognise your phone in *Watch together* | Only to phones on your local network that are looking for it. |
| **Settings** (chosen speakers, volumes, equalizer, pairing keys for AirPlay devices) | To remember your choices | Stored only on your phone. Deleted when you uninstall the app. |
| **Recordings** (only if you turn on *Record to a file*) | Saving a copy of what you stream | Saved only on your phone, in the app's own folder. |

## What SoundBeam does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share personal data with the developer or any third party.
- It has no servers and does not send data to the internet.
- It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
- It does not require an account.

## Data security

Audio is sent directly between your phone and your devices over your local network. AirPlay 2 audio is encrypted by the AirPlay protocol; other protocols (DLNA, Sonos, HEOS, Chromecast, AriaCast and SoundBeam Live) send audio unencrypted on your local network, as those systems require.

## Children

SoundBeam is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Your choices

You can revoke notification access, microphone access or any other permission at any time in Android's settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published in the project repository with an updated date above.
